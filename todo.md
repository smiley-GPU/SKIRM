## Wound system
Create more abstract damage system for player to see. You can still use number mechanics behind the screen.
Light wounds, cut and bruises = cause minor negative effect, and you can take few of these. Three if that is balanced.
Major injury= broken finger, broken ribs, sprained tendon or similar. gives a slightly larger negative modifier, you can only have one of these
Critical injury = broken arm, leg, concussion, major bleeding. bigger negative effect. needs tending before getting worse.
Lethal = character unconsious, will die if not tended.

## Interludes and character personalities
Insertion, after lift-off but before entry to ops site, and extraction, after leaving but before erturning base, should have interludes where each character comments the coming misssion or done mission based on their personality.
Create 20 different personalities, and assign alzways one to a team member.
In Insertion each charaacter comment the coming mission, their expectations about it, their gear or intel or similar in the way their personality would.
Then player chooses a one of these statements. This could be "Whos words linger in your mind after the silence comes to the cabin/silent walking starts/appropriate text based on vehicles or so".
This chosen statement should reflect in the mission somehow. If a team member is worried about certain adversary, then that adversary should create some extra problems to the team, not necessary a stronger enemy, or more accurate or damaging. Or maybe he could return after a kll-shot, in a " I am not dead yet" way. Or if operator is proud of his new gun, then that gun should have bit extra power in the mission (first use or so).
In the extraction interlude, team members should think what just happened, if a downing an enemy was a remarkable, or blowing something up, or helping a friend.
Player again chooses one of the statements. That would then somehow realize to a game effect. If someone helped an another operator then they could have a bond between them that gives extra benefit. If a gun was very effective, then that could change to be a signature gun. If a fellow operator died, then could give survivor some grit.

Think what you would need to implelent this kind of mechanic: personalities, comment line for personalities, different things to ponder about, effects for this line, mechanical construction of choosing etc.
